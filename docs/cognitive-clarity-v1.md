# Ask ReveNew — Cognitive Clarity V1

## Objective

Make the existing working surface easier to scan, read and trust without
introducing a redesign or another visual system.

This pass deliberately makes `comfortable` density the default rather than
adding a density toggle. The target buyer is making commercial decisions, not
performing high-volume table administration.

## Changes

- larger drawer-safe executive headline (22–26px, not 32–36px);
- 14–15px main explanatory copy;
- microcopy lifted out of the 8–9px range;
- more whitespace around question, decision hero and findings;
- full answer, evidence and limitations remain collapsed by default;
- follow-ups capped at two;
- commercial facts condensed to four executive metrics in a two-column band;
- issue detail becomes `De ce contează` + `Ce faci acum`;
- explanation and source facts move behind `Context și dovezi`;
- Context Integrity receives more breathing room and larger labels;
- human resolution options become easier to read;
- secondary actions stay visually secondary;
- fewer hard divider lines; spacing carries more of the hierarchy.

## What does not change

- Context Integrity evaluation;
- finding persistence;
- human resolution semantics;
- database/RPC behavior;
- CRM/document/provider mutations;
- evidence provenance;
- audit history;
- Ask ReveNew backend.

## Why not 32–36px?

The screenshots show a ~600px decision drawer. A 32–36px answer headline would
consume too much vertical area and wrap aggressively. The chosen 22–26px range
creates clear executive hierarchy while preserving the decision context on
screen.

## Freeze rule

After visual QA, this surface should be frozen again. Further work should move
to the operational Recovery Queue and measurable execution outcomes.
